# Batch 5 triage: Sahih al-Bukhari 4250–4473 and Sahih Muslim 1730–1817

This is the last Sahih batch. Battles cannot hold reports, so a story inside a battle or a campaign (Mu'tah, the conquest,
Hunayn, al-Ta'if, Tabuk) is its own event. Long stories are told whole: Ka'b ibn Malik (4418) and Abu Sufyan before
Heraclius (Muslim 1773). Mu'allaq reports are kept only where al-Bukhari gives them in committal form («وقال فلان»).

Hatib's letter: 4274 (Ali, al-Zubayr and al-Miqdad, with «لعل الله اطلع على من شهد بدرا» and 60:1 sent down) is fuller than
3983, so it now leads the-letter-of-hatib, and 3983 follows it.

The farewell hajj sermon (4406, 4402, 4403, 4405) and Umar's verse of Arafah (4407) are their own events. hajjat-al-wada has
no cited year, so it stays an event and off the «حياته» timeline (`src/lib/catalog/appLife.test.ts`).

The reports on his illness and death go to the existing passing-of-the-prophet-muhammad event. The scenes that stand
alone are their own events: the day of Thursday, Fatimah weeps and laughs, the medicine in his mouth, the last look at the
prayer, al-Abbas and Ali, and Abu Bakr on the day of his death.

Our copy lacks 13 numbers in this range: 4306, 4308, 4319, 4327, 4342, 4345, 4354, 4434, 4444, 4453, 4456, 4457 and 4465.
Muslim 1817 is a story, but in our copy the next kitab's heading runs into its end, so it is left as a data issue.

each one, check whether al-Bukhari also tells it in that chapter (3571 onward) when the tag is built.
