## Signs of prophethood: candidates for the tag (not added)

Owner answer 4: a story takes the tag only when its source files it under such a chapter. These are plainly signs; whether
al-Bukhari also tells each under «علامات النبوة» (3571 onward) is to be checked when the tag is built.

- jabirs-food-at-the-trench (4101, 4102)
- the-well-of-al-hudaybiyah (4150, 4151); the arrow in the well inside 2731 (treaty-of-hudaybiyyah) and the well in Muslim 1807
- water-from-his-fingers-at-al-hudaybiyah (4152; Jabir's same hadith is told in «علامات النبوة»)
- the-banner-at-khaybar (4210: he spat in Ali's eyes and they healed)
- the-wound-of-salamah-at-khaybar (4206)
- the-man-of-the-fire (4202, 4207) and the-din-and-the-wicked-man (4203): what he foretold came true, «صدق الله حديثك»
- the-judgement-of-saad-ibn-muadh (4122): Sa'd's du'a answered (weaker)
