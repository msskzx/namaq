Done 2026-10-04 by an agent from web search and page fetches. Nothing here is verified. "Page seen" means a page was fetched and a summarizing tool reported what it said; I did not read the pages myself, and nothing was checked against a printed book. A search-result description is not a check either. "Unverified" means a search result or memory only. No Arabic text was read for fidelity, and no edition below has been compared with print. Conclusion: Shamela lists a Bukhari and a Muslim, so Shamela-only may be possible. The Bukhari edition identity is not established: Shamela's description names the Bulaq 1311 AH (Sultaniyya) text and the Dar Tawq al-Najat 1422 AH edition, which may or may not be separate printings (the catalogue says Tawq al-Najat is based on the Bulaq edition, so "different printings" is my inference and unchecked); which one the digital text follows is open. Three things are open: whether the digital text matches print (needs a page-image comparison of a sample, starting with Bukhari 50), whether any scholar reviewed the digital text, and whether re-hosting is permitted. The last needs the rights holders or Shamela's contact, not more searching.
