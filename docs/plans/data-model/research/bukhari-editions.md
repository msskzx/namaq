# Which edition of Sahih al-Bukhari to extract from

Researched 2026-10-03. Page facts come from WebFetch, which summarizes a page through a small model rather than returning it verbatim. Treat every "quote" below as a paraphrase until someone opens the page and checks it. Anything not seen on an opened page is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## Candidates

### 1. Sultaniyya (Bulaq 1311 AH), reproduced by Dar Tawq al-Najah (Zuhayr al-Nasir), 1422 AH
- Editor: the Bulaq committee of scholars (al-Azhar). Dr Muhammad Zuhayr al-Nasir reissued it in 2001. Source: Shamela card 1681.
- Publisher: Dar Tawq al-Najah, Beirut, 1st ed. 1422 AH / 2001 CE, 9 parts. The original was printed at al-Matba'a al-Kubra al-Amiriyya, Bulaq, 1311 AH. Source: Shamela 1681.
- Basis: al-Yunini's copy, preserved in Istanbul. Shamela page 1681/2 names "النسخة اليونينية". The Bulaq margin carries al-Yunini's riwaya variants with symbols (**UNVERIFIED** on Tawq's digital text).
- Numbering: the Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi numbers were added. ketabonline 2130 says "مصورة عن السلطانية بإضافة ترقيم ... محمد فؤاد عبد الباقي". Shamela 1681 says the pagination matches the printed edition.
- Vowelled: yes (Shamela offers a vowelling toggle).
- Footnotes: the publisher added takhrij references and, according to ketabonline 2130, al-Bugha's explanatory notes. Whether Shamela's text keeps the Yunini variant marks is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Scholarly review: the 1311 committee is famous. No preface statement was quoted (**UNVERIFIED**).
- Digital: Shamela https://shamela.ws/book/1681; scans on archive.org (`jsnum`, a coloured Yunini printing credited to al-Nasir and Tawq).
- Known problems: none confirmed (**UNVERIFIED**).

### 2. Al-Yunini text from Ata'at al-Ilm (Bukhari-pedia), 1437 AH
- Producer: the Ata'at al-Ilm company, funded by the Sulaiman al-Rajhi foundation. Source: bukhari-pedia.net.
- Basis: al-Yunini's recension, with symbols for each riwaya: (س) Ibn Asakir, (هـ) Abu Dharr al-Harawi, (ص) al-Asili, and others. Source: Shamela 1376 and page 1376/2.
- Numbering: pages follow the Bayt al-Sunnah edition ("رقمنا صفحات الكتاب موافقة لطبعة بيت السنة"). Hadith numbering is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Vowelled: yes. Footnotes: riwaya variants (the strongest match to our need).
- Review: the site cites endorsements from Dr Sa'd al-Khathlan, Dr Sa'd al-Humayd and Dr Khaldun al-Ahdab. These praise the project and are not a proof-reading statement.
- Digital: Shamela https://shamela.ws/book/1376; bukhari-pedia.net.
- Terms (bukhari-pedia): all rights reserved; it forbids copying for commercial purposes and republishing its books on other websites. **Namaq's use would need permission.**

### 3. Dar al-Ta'sil, Cairo, 1433 AH / 2012
- Committee: Dar al-Ta'sil's research centre (names **UNVERIFIED**). Shamela 1284 says it was checked against the Sultaniyya edition, with its symbols explained.
- 10 volumes (9 plus indices). Pagination matches print.
- Vowelling, footnotes and numbering: **UNVERIFIED**.
- Digital: Shamela https://shamela.ws/book/1284.

### 4. Mustafa Dib al-Bugha, Dar Ibn Kathir / Dar al-Yamama, Damascus, 5th ed. 1414 AH / 1993
- 7 volumes, the last holding indices. Pagination matches print. Source: Shamela 735.
- Numbering: al-Bugha's own sequence (about 7,563), which differs from Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi's (**UNVERIFIED**, from general knowledge).
- Footnotes: explanatory, and he adds his own vowelling (per the search snippet). The basis manuscripts are **UNVERIFIED**.
- Digital: Shamela https://shamela.ws/book/735; archive.org `sbkhbugha`.

### 5. Printed with Fath al-Bari (Dar al-Ma'rifa / Salafiyya, Muhibb al-Din al-Khatib), Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi numbering
- Dar al-Ma'rifa, Beirut, 1379 AH, 13 volumes. Kitab, bab and hadith are numbered by Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi, and al-Khatib corrected it (search snippet; Shamela 1673 is the Salafiyya printing). The matn is Ibn Hajar's riwaya (Abu Dharr), not the Yunini consensus text (**UNVERIFIED**).
- Footnotes: commentary rather than variant apparatus. Not suited to a matn-only extraction.
- Digital: https://shamela.ws/book/1673 (not opened).

### 6. Dar al-Salam, Riyadh (single volume, 1999; also in Mawsu'at al-Kutub al-Sitta, 2008)
- The Mawsu'a is supervised by Salih Al al-Shaykh. The search snippet gives over 2,700 pages for the six books. Basis, vowelling, footnotes and numbering are all **UNVERIFIED**.
- Digital: PDF scans only (archive.org `bukhari-DarSalam-ed`, not opened). Not on Shamela.

### 7. Dorar.net and Islamweb
- Dorar: both pages returned 403, so nothing about it is verified. It is a graded search database, not an edition.
- Islamweb library: the Bukhari page names no editor or publisher. The footer reads "جميع الحقوق محفوظة" (all rights reserved). Unusable as a cited edition until its source edition is identified.

### Waqfeya
- Hosts PDF scans (seen in results for Fath al-Bari). These are scans, not extractable text, but useful for checking a text against print.

## Recommendation (confidence: medium)

1. **Sultaniyya via Tawq al-Najah (Shamela 1681).** It is the Yunini text the scholars already use, and it carries the Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi numbers that the world cites. The 1311 base text is public domain, though the 2001 additions may not be.
2. **Ata'at al-Ilm Yunini (Shamela 1376)**, if the riwaya-variant footnotes matter most. Its terms forbid republishing, so a licence is needed.
3. Dar al-Ta'sil, as a cross-check. Avoid al-Bugha (non-standard numbering) and Fath al-Bari printings (a different matn).

## Numbering to store

Store the **Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi hadith number** (with kitab/bab numbers) beside the printed volume and page. Two printings use it, Tawq al-Najah and the Fath al-Bari printings, and it is the scheme the top candidate carries. Store an edition-specific number only as a secondary field. How Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi numbers repeats and mu'allaq reports is **UNVERIFIED**.

## What the owner and scholars must verify

- Open Shamela 1681 pages and check whether the Yunini variant symbols and Tawq's footnotes survive in the digital text.
- Read the Tawq al-Najah and Ata'at prefaces for who proof-read the text, and record the page.
- Check the Dar al-Ta'sil committee, numbering and footnotes (Shamela 1284).
- Decide whether 1376's hadith numbering is Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi's.
- Get written reuse permission from Tawq al-Najah or Ata'at al-Ilm, or a legal view that the base text is public domain.
- Collect known errata for the chosen edition (none found).
- Scholars: confirm the chosen edition is the one they accept for vowelling and riwaya.
