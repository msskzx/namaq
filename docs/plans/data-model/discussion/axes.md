DECISION AXES (the same list for all four agents; neutral wording, no side preferred):
X1 Where text lives and how a displayed string is produced (resolved from a source span vs copied and checked).
X2 Span identity: how a span survives a re-fetch, re-split or repagination; re-anchoring.
X3 What replaces catalog values and claim.assertion: the typed statement/assertion object, and what a plain Siyar claim costs.
X4 Reports, chains, links and the mode-of-hearing vocabulary; gaps (muallaq, mursal); chain-less and anonymous opinions.
X5 Entities, mentions and identification: global entity per person, who decides an identification, its cited basis, who reviews.
X6 Variants and traditions: grouping reports without merging texts; who may group.
X7 Gradings and judgments: how a grade is held as a quotation of its speaker; author frame (a work's own claim).
X8 Approval and publication granularity and hashing; what lapses when text changes.
X9 Storage and projection; the fate of data/catalog, batches, seeds, PostgreSQL tables and the Neo4j graph (narrators as nodes).
X10 Migration phasing, what ships first (Siyar conversion vs hadith first), rollback.
X11 Editions, hosts and numbering (Shamela only? bare Sira volumes, Bukhari edition and numbering standard, Ayah/mushaf).
X12 Review process and cost (who reviews, stricter gate for Prophetic statements, effort per record).
X13 Visibility: what is shown while unreviewed or legacy (vs ADR 0008 and the 'no unreferenced text' principle).
