# Branch category review

The category manifest records an agent editorial assessment of the complete generated comparison inventory on 2026-11-06. It does not claim exhaustive scholarly review or human approval. Category membership does not supply evidence for a grammatical chip.

## Coverage

All 1,141 existing generated comparison IDs appear exactly once in the manifest's rule groups. The review inspected the selected wording for 540 computed near-wording comparisons, 509 shared-expression comparisons, 91 exact repeats and one messenger-frame comparison. The generated `kind` fields served as review batches; they do not determine the published categories.

Before deduplication and the additional focused selections, 833 comparisons receive mutashabeh, 311 receive pondering, and 289 remain word trees. There are 292 comparisons in both of the first two categories, so the default inventory contains 852 distinct comparisons. Runtime scope filtering and duplicate removal must determine the final displayed count.

The manifest also supplies two focused comparisons in both default categories and one broad word tree. They are separate from the 1,141 inventory counts. Every new comparison absent from the manifest remains a word tree until assessed.

## Decisions

- Mutashabeh includes uncited comparisons whose selected clauses or sentence constructions make useful recall comparisons. A source reference is not required.
- A shared opening can qualify when it gives a focused point at which a learner must distinguish subsequent wording. An unrelated common ending or a clipped alignment alone is insufficient.
- Exact repetition can help distinguish surrounding recall contexts. Short chapter letters and small formula fragments do not automatically qualify.
- Pondering comparisons have an identifiable construction, narrative, instruction, contrast or described sign. These labels record a comparison of the visible text, not an inferred tafsir conclusion.
- Broad formulas, weakly joined alignment fragments and uncertain comparisons remain word trees. This is an exploration choice, not a claim that the ayat have no meaningful relationship.
- Source cases may classify an entire comparison while their citations support only particular member selections. Difference chips remain restricted to those source-supported selections.

## Maintenance

The rule groups contain explicit legacy IDs and reasons. They are reviewed data, not runtime thresholds. Existing URL aliases resolve them after canonical IDs are introduced. A change to passage membership or word spans needs category review because the old reason may no longer describe the new comparison.

The review preserves the full generated inventory under the word-tree option. It neither removes Quran passages nor treats a missing source citation as evidence that a book excludes a comparison.
