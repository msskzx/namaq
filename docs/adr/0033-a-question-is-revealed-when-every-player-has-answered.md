# A question is revealed when every player has answered

A party is for memorizing together, so it waits for its players instead of
rewarding the fastest. After a player submits, their device confirms that the
party has the answer but not whether it is right. The reveal comes when every
player has submitted or the host's timer for the question runs out, and shows
the correct answer, each player's result and the leaderboard. The host then
moves the party to the next question. The final leaderboard follows the last
reveal.

Players have no account and type a name to join. The party does not guard
against cheating. A player who looks up an answer only loses the practice. The
host plays like everyone else and also holds the party's controls.

## Rules the owner set on 2026-10-26

- The host sets the timer, with a longer default for shared-openings questions
  than for other questions. A player who has not submitted when it runs out
  scores nothing on that question.
- The host may start before every player is ready, after confirming, so one
  player who missed the ready button does not hold up the rest.
- No one joins after the party starts.
- A device that drops out rejoins as the same player with its score.
- When the host leaves, the player who joined next after them becomes host,
  skipping players whose devices are away. A host whose device drops out for
  30 seconds is replaced the same way.
- Names are unique within a party, and the host can remove a player in the
  lobby. A removed device cannot join that party again.

## Considered options

- Showing each player their result right after they submit. Players who have
  finished could then call out answers to those still typing.
- Moving on automatically after a fixed pause. The host knows when the group
  has finished looking at an answer, and a tree correction can take a while to
  read.
