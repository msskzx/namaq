# 0036. A word written differently moves to its neighbour

Since [0035](0035-a-tree-node-shows-words-only-as-the-mushaf-writes-them.md) a node drew a word its ayat write differently once for each form, stacked in an amber bracket. On 231 of the 1,056 default trees the bracket made one node look split in two, often for a mark the next or previous word causes: the shadda of مِّلَّةَ in al-An'am 161 comes from the tanween of قِيَمًا before it, and the madd of رَبَّنَآ in Al Imran 16 comes from the hamza after it.

The owner chose (2026-11-14) to move such a word into the neighbouring nodes when they already part the ayat the same way. The first word of the trunk joins the words before it in each ayah; the last word of a node joins its children. Each neighbour then writes the word as its own ayat do, in amber. A one-word inner node dissolves into its children; the trunk always keeps at least one word. This takes one word per move and handles 176 of the 234 bracketed nodes.

A word the neighbours cannot take (in the middle of a node, or where the neighbours part the ayat another way or an ayah ends) stays in its node and is drawn as a braid: the line parts into one strand per way of writing, each with its ayat, and the strands meet again. Neighbouring differing words share one braid, so عَلَيْكُمُ ٱلْمَيْتَةَ وَٱلدَّمَ وَلَحْمَ and ٱلْمَيْتَةُ وَٱلدَّمُ وَلَحْمُ make one fork of three words, not three brackets. About 50 trees keep a braid.

`foldForms` in `shared/quran/foldForms.ts` moves the words, and `phrasePresentation` applies it, so the website's branches page, the reader's previews and the app's tree cards all draw the folded tree. Quiz questions and the subtree inventory build from `createPhrasePresenter` unchanged. `writtenPieces` and `writtenStrands` in `shared/quran/writtenForms.ts` decide the braids; the website and the app both draw from them. Every drawn word is still written that way in its ayat (0035), and a test checks that each moved word sits at its own place in each ayah.
