# Classify the role of cited evidence

A consulted historical work can contain a direct record, a transmitted earlier
report, the author's synthesis, or a modern editor's analysis. We record that
evidence role on the citation instead of labeling an entire work “primary” or
“secondary.” New canonical changes require pinpoint citations to eligible
historical works, but they do not require a surviving contemporary document.
Digital hosts such as Shamela and Islamweb remain delivery locations, not evidence
classes.

Strict primary-only admission would exclude much of the transmitted material on
which Namaq relies. Treating every historical work alike would conceal meaningful
differences in how a passage supports a claim.
