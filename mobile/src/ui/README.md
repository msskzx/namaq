# Shared pieces (app)

Screens arrange these; they never draw their own controls or boxes
(AGENTS.md "Shared first", guard `shared/sharedFirst.test.ts`). Need something
new? Add an option to the piece here, or build a new piece here and list it
below. Never copy one into a screen.

| Piece | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Button` (+ `Row`) | Filled, outlined or plain button with an icon; `pill` for round ends; `Row` wraps a row of them | every tap that reads as a button, icon-only included (`variant="plain"`) |
| `IconButton` | Icon-only button: `bar` (22 in 40) or `small` (18 in 28), tone muted/ink/accent/green, `on` colours the icon (no square), `round` filled accent circle for a bar's main action, `dot`, `expanded`, `role="link"` | every button that shows only an icon |
| `OpenButton` | A home card's one open button: the «افتح» pill with the forward arrow (Button `pill`; the word first, then its icon, as on every button), `label` says where for a screen reader (owner, 2026-11-22) | first in every home card's row; a long card with no page opens whole with it (`CardWithRow` `cap`) |
| `CardShare` (+ `CardPicture`) | A home card's share button: the card as one picture, its head, the card as it stands with no buttons (`UnderCard` goes nowhere), its books written out in full by `PictureRefs`: places and printing (owner, 2026-11-22; release 1) | every home card without a share of its own (`Post`'s default) |
| `SharedCard` (+ `ShareHead`, `NoShare`) | A page's section (`CardWithRow`) with the share button last in its row, its picture under `ShareHead` naming the page; none inside another card or under `NoShare` (a quiz's look-up) | hadith, Forty, Sahih, person, battle and adhkar cards; `Section`'s `share` |
| `ActButton` | Icon with its name under it; coloured icon when on; `wide` for a longer name; `quran` sets the name in the mushaf font; `onPressAt` hands where the button is (to open a bubble over it), `expanded` while that is open | long-press and card menus, the home feed cards' «…» menu |
| `TopBar` | Back (or the hub grid, or the shelf) first on the right, then `title` or `middle`, then the page buttons; help at the far left; the logo beside the gear on home only; see-through, hides on scroll | every screen's top bar, pushed screens included |
| `IconTile` | A hub's way into one feature: its icon from `components/featureIcons` over its name, two to a row; `wide` lays the icon beside the name across the row, with one muted `line` | the Quran and Tarajim tabs |
| `BarPicker` | A top bar's dropdown: text, chevron, no border; optional icon and count | the open list or item in a top bar (timeline, saved lists, compare, Tuhfa) |
| `PostHead` | A home card's head: round icon, title, a muted line under it; `open` makes it one button to the card's general topic (dims on press, no chevron) | every feed card, and each section of a page drawn as home cards (the ayah page, the saved list) |
| `AyahMenuPress` | A long press on an ayah view opens the reader's menu on its ayah (or asks which of its ayat); off inside a `Sheet` (`ayahMenuContext.ts`) | `AyahFlow`, `AyahLines` (components/AyahMenuHost.tsx draws the menu) |
| `Card` | The one card: radius 14, one colour, rule border; `pad` (or [top and bottom, sides]), `gap`, `spine`, `dashed`, `outline`, `picked`, `faded`, `clip`; with `onPress` the whole card is one button; a `Card` inside a card draws flat (no fill, no frame, a rule above), and `useInsideCard()` tells a self-framed piece (the matrix) to draw no frame. `CardWithRow` is a card with the row of buttons under it and the book line under the row (the feed post): `lead` first in the row, `row` after it, `menu` its «⋯» at the row's far left, `source` the books on the line (a `Reference` inside lifts itself there), `cap` stopping long content at two thirds of the screen with a fade, the whole in a sheet from `OpenButton` when the card has no `lead`; a feature's own buttons inside it go there with `UnderCard` (`useCardRow()` says they will). Drawn inside another card it draws no card of its own: its buttons join that card's row and its books that card's line. The website has the same in `src/components/common/CardWithRow.tsx` (`card` for the card's own id and attributes) | every boxed block of content (feed posts, every section of a page, shelves, books, quizzes, Quran tools, sheets, share images) |
| `Tabs` | The website's segmented row: framed, the open tab filled in the accent; `fill` stretches, `iconOnly` | a few views of one thing (read or recite, two books, contents/saved/search, the two kinds of oaths); a wrapping list of words or filters is chips |
| `StripTabs` | A strip of many short tabs over pages swiped sideways: right to left, the open one underlined in the accent and kept in view, each at least 44 wide; `names` read out | the years of «قصص السيرة» |
| `Chip` | A round framed word to pick in a wrapping row, filled in the accent when on; `lit` (+`tint`) stays framed for Quran words and surahs, `quran`, `check`, `dashed`, `small`, `count`, `onClear` (removable), `Icon` (an icon before the label: «+ أضف» after removable chips) | filters, settings with several values, topics, an ayah's words, a tree's surahs, a search's terms |
| `Field` (+ `SearchField`) | The one text field: framed like a card, 44 tall, 16px, right-aligned; `multiline` for a note, `code` for a party's number; `SearchField` adds the magnifier and a clear button | every typed input except the recite writing boxes |
| `RowScroll` | A sideways row of chips or cards that starts on the right with its first item, whatever the platform; `gap`, `bleed` (reaches to the screen's edges) | the home interest chips, the books post, any sideways row |
| `Tap` | The one press: a finger that moved more than 10px (a scroll or swipe) never presses | inside every button piece and tappable card; a screen drawing one counts as a control |
| `motion` (`useSlideAway`, `useHidden`, `hiddenProps`) | The one hide motion: slide off the edge and fade, hide 200ms ease-in, show 260ms ease-out | every bar that hides or comes back |
| `GlassBar` | A floating frosted bar of buttons, as tall as one icon button; `small` is a shorter pill sized to small buttons, sharing its row | recite mode's bar (`components/recite/RecitePill`; `small` in a quiz row) |
| `TabBar`, `tabBarSpace` | The floating bottom tab bar and its height | room under content |
| `column` | The tablet column (680pt), home's two columns lying down, `usePageWidth` for anything sized from the page, `isTablet`/`isWide` | sizing from the window width |
| `FloatingMenu` (+ `MenuAt`) | A row of ActButtons over what was pressed, wrapping, above or below the finger; `groups` lays them in groups with a thin line between; `bubble` (+ `span`, the button's height) is the round reaction pop-up over one button of an open menu; `overlay` dims behind and closes on a tap outside | the mushaf and book long press, the home cards menu |
| `MoreMenu` | A card's «⋯»: an icon button that opens a `FloatingMenu` of its choices (ActButtons); nothing when there is none | the feed cards' «ليس من اهتماماتي» |
| `Sheet` | Rises from the bottom, tap outside or swipe down closes; `tall` one height above the keyboard, `full` whole screen with title and close | every sheet, full-screen ones included (share, video) |
| `Toggle` | On means knob on the right | every on/off setting |
| `OrderRows` | Rows to drag into order, the row following the finger (at once from its grip, after a short hold elsewhere) and the others making room; then «تحقق»; once answered, the right order with where each was put | a question that puts its choices in order («رتّب السور») |
| `OrderTray` | Numbered boxes in mushaf order filled from a tray of names: a name tapped fills the lit box, a filled box tapped sends its name back, an empty one becomes the next; «تحقق» at any time; once checked, green, accent with the struck name, or «لم تضعها» | a long order («رتّب سور الجزء», the surah-order page's practice) |
| `NumberPicker` (+ `NumberList`) | Drop-down number list | picking a number (no steppers) |
| `Text` | Amiri text with the interface scale | every non-Quran text |
| `ExternalLink` (+ `openExternal`) | Opens a page outside the app | every link out of Namaq |
| `Reference` | A book's quote with its page as a link; inside a `CardWithRow` it draws nothing and joins the card's line | a section's sources, handed up to its card's line |
| `SourceLine` | A home card's source line under its buttons: a book icon and the books, a tap opening a sheet with where in them (a book cited in several places named once, `mergeSources`); `named` defaults to all of `sources`; `sourcesOf` turns a question's `QuizSource[]` into its entries. A `Reference` inside a `CardWithRow` hands itself up to the line (`sourceSlot.ts`) | every home card and page section (owner 2026-11-20); alone under a page's heading for the heading's own book |
| `Folded` | A fold of more content under a rule at its card's foot (not a credit: a book goes on the card's line) | other routes and reports of a hadith or story; the ayat a balagha quote fits when al-Suyuti names none |
| `SettingRow` | A settings row that folds: icon, name, what is chosen; a tap opens the picker under it | settings' «ما أحفظه» and «اهتماماتي» |
| `TapRow` | A frameless row of a drawing: a tap opens what it names, a long press shows its source; 44 tall | the rows of the «حياته» timeline; a year's column on the battles timeline (`style` turns it into a column) |
| `Pickable` | A block of a card that picks it on a tap, `aria-pressed` | a book's paragraph that lights the ayah words it quotes |
| `Saying` | Someone's words with who says them on a muted line before them («قال فلان:»); `by` left out draws the words alone; `center` centres both | every quote from a book with its sayer (a person's virtues, a laqab's words, a tie's sentence) |
| `Points` | Separate sayings or facts one under another, each with an accent point at its first line | a sayer's several sayings under one «قال فلان:» (a person's virtues) |
| `FactCells` | Short facts side by side, ruled apart, each a small label with its icon and every reading with who gives it | a person's death, place and age on the person card |
| `QuoteBar` | A saying with a bar down its right, its sayer first in words, chosen words lit in the accent (`name`) or on a soft band (`mark`); `quoted` draws it between «» with no bar; `foot` under the words; `children` for words drawn by their own piece (a book's text with its Quran quotes) | a person's virtues and looks (the Prophet's ﷺ in amber); the ayah page's «قال السعدي:» |
| `SayingBand` | One speaker's sayings on a tinted band at the head of their card, his name once above them | the Prophet's ﷺ words first in a person's «مناقبه» |
| `TraitChips` | Framed words in a wrapping row, who gives each under it; one two named sayers give framed in the accent; `quran` and `meaning` for an ayah's words, `onPick` to make them tabs | a person's «صفته»; the ayah page's «كلماتها» |
| `Bubble` | A speech bubble in the speaker's colour, on his side, his corner sharp, the lead-in above it; no name ring (owner, 2026-11-22); `framed` | a conversation drawn as a chat (the dialog scenes of «مشاهد», a hadith's scenes, a sira story drawn by speaker through `HadithScene`) |
| `StepDots` | A row of dots (`column` stands them up) for the steps of something walked in order: the reached one bigger in the theme's yellow, passed ones muted; a tap goes to a step | the layers of a «مشاهد» scene; a story's shared pictures in the share sheet |
| `Shade` | A soft dark shade behind words over a picture, round at the corners; takes an animated style | the ayah and the sayings over a «مشاهد» scene's sky |
| `Step` | A numbered step: the number in an accent ring, its content beside it | a sequence told in order (a battle's moments) |
| `BarStrip` | A row of bars, the first on the right, each as tall as its count, the whole one control: a touch or slide picks the nearest bar and names it in a pill above the finger; `onLift` acts on lifting, `mark` points at one, `ends` names the two ends, `holdScroll` | where a word gathers by surah (`SurahStrip`), a Sahih's kitabs by their hadith (`SahihShape`) |
| `NumberGrid` | Numbers in rings, six to a row, each opening its own; `tone` rings them in a colour | al-Nawawi's Forty and Ibn Rajab's eight on the hadith tab |
| `Ring` | A small ring on its card's paper holding a number or an icon, in a tone | a Sahih's kitab numbers on their line |
| `ShareBar` | A thin bar from the start, as long as its share of the row | how big a kitab is beside the longest |
| `SplitBar` | Two amounts on one bar, each its share, accent then rule colour | comparing two sides (a battle's numbers) |
| `Empty` | The one "nothing here" line: muted, centred, says what is missing and how to fill it | every empty list or search |
| `LoadingDots`, `LoadingScreen`, `Hourglass` | The one loader (`fill` centres it for a page or sheet, `label` for screen readers), app start screen, quiz timer | waiting |
