# صفية بنت حيي

This batch preserves Safiyyah bint Huyayy's complete entry from _Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'_, volume 2, pages 231–238. The entry identifies her as a Mother of the Believers and records her lineage, beauty, character, and marriage to the Prophet.

The extraction begins at the entry heading on page 231, after two paragraphs from Hafsah's entry, and ends before Maymunah's heading on page 238. The matching editorial notes were trimmed at the same boundaries.

The catalog now cites the source for Safiyyah's full name, appearance, virtues, titles, three marriage relations, father relation, and the selected death-year report. The batch adds Huyayy ibn Akhtab, Sallam ibn Abi al-Huqayq, and Kinanah ibn Abi al-Huqayq as graph-only catalog people so the cited relations have targets. Safiyyah's sex and the three graph-only people's sex remain `legacy-unreviewed`. The entry gives no kunya. It mentions an unnamed Jewish brother in a bequest report, which is preserved in the source page but cannot support an identified catalog relation.

The entry gives competing death years, 36 AH and 50 AH. Both reports remain visible as disputed claims; the catalog selects 50 AH.

The claims remain `NOT_REVIEWED`. Publication approval and human review are separate actions.
