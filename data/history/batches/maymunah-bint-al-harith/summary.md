# Maimuna bint al-Harith

This batch preserves al-Dhahabi's complete entry for Maimuna bint al-Harith from volume 5, printed pages 238–245. It records her lineage, marriage to the Prophet, sisterhood with Umm al-Fadl, virtues, and Khalifa's report that she died in 51 AH.

Maimuna is in scope as a Companion because the entry identifies her as a Mother of the Believers and wife of the Prophet. The existing Companion title remains `legacy-unreviewed`: the account supports her status through the marriage but does not state the title صحابية directly.

The entry begins midway through printed page 238. Extraction starts at `p4`, the numbered heading, and trims Safiyya's preceding body text. The shared footnote block starts at `مسند أحمد`, preserving Maimuna's bibliography while dropping Safiyya's notes. Printed page 245 ends Maimuna's entry, so no end trim was needed.

Two earlier husbands are preserved in the source text but are not modeled because neither man has a catalog subject. The rejected 61 AH death report remains in the account; the catalog takes Khalifa's 51 AH report because al-Dhahabi explicitly rejects the later date.

Claims remain `NOT_REVIEWED`, and the batch has no publication approval.
