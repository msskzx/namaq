# Batch: Aisha bint Abi Bakr, Siyar entry 19

This batch preserves al-Dhahabi's complete entry on Aisha bint Abi Bakr and
supports the canonical values selected from it. It follows the
[data quality and references workflow](../../../../docs/data-pipelines.md).

- Batch definition: [batch.json](batch.json)
- Source pages: [accounts/aisha-bint-abi-bakr/](accounts/aisha-bint-abi-bakr/)

## Source account

Entry 19 of *Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'*, Risalah third edition (1405/1985),
volume 5, edited by Hussein Asad under Shuayb al-Arnaut. The account starts
with its heading on printed page 135 and ends on page 201. Neither boundary
includes text from a neighboring entry, so no anchor or editorial-note trimming
was needed.

The heading and opening identify Aisha as a wife of the Prophet and Mother of
the Believers. She is in scope as a Companion.

## What the entry supports

Ten claims select values represented by the catalog model. The opening supplies
her full nasab, daughter relation to Abu Bakr, marriage to the Prophet, Mother
of the Believers title, and distinction in knowledge. Page 140 supplies her
appearance. A report on page 181 calls her `الصديقة بنت الصديق`. The account
also identifies Abd al-Rahman as her brother, connects her vindication to
Quran 24:11, and records her response to Quran 33:33.

The appearance and virtues fields now use wording fully supported by this
account. The retired seed's statement that she was thin does not occur in the
entry and was removed rather than carried without evidence.

## Checklist and legacy values

The entry gives no kunya, so the account marks `kunya` absent. It covers her
nasab, appearance, virtues, marriage, and siblings. The generic Companion title
and sex remain inherited from the retired seeds. Every other scoped legacy
value supported by this entry is promoted to a cited claim.

## Review

Nothing is marked reviewed. All claims remain `NOT_REVIEWED`, and this batch
has no publication approval.
