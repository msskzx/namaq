# Summary: جبر بن عتيك (Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', vol 5, pp. 36-37)

## Source
**Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'** by al-Dhahabi, vol 5 (سير أعلام النبلاء ج٢), pp. 36-37. Edition: دار الرسالة, 3rd ed. 1405/1985. Digital: المكتبة الشاملة (shamela.ws/book/10906/2016 for p. 36, /2017 for p. 37). Shamela's الجزء ٢ is this edition's volume 5 (its الجزء count skips the two sira volumes and the caliph volume bound before the Siyar proper).

## Extracted Content
The entry spans two printed pages. Page 36 holds 14 paragraphs:

1. **Archer** (p1): "وَكَانَ أَحَدَ الرُّمَاةِ المَوْصُوْفِيْنَ" — noted as one of the renowned archers.
2. **Death year 42 AH** (p2): "وَقِيْلَ: تُوُفِّيَ سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْنِ وَأَرْبَعِيْنَ" — a reported death year.
3. **Nasab part 1** (p3): "٧ - جَبْرُ بنُ عَتِيْكِ بنِ قَيْسِ بنِ هَيْشَةَ بنِ الحَارِثِ الأَنْصَارِيُّ" — opening nasab.
4. **Nasab part 2 + Kunya** (p4): "ابْنِ أُمَيَّةَ بنِ مُعَاوِيَةَ بنِ مَالِكِ بنِ عَوْفِ بنِ عَمْرِو بنِ عَوْفٍ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، أَبُو عَبْدِ اللهِ" — completes nasab and gives kunya.
5. **Badr veteran** (p5): "بَدْرِيٌّ كَبِيْرٌ" — designated a major Badr participant.
6. **Name dispute** (p6): "وَقِيْلَ: اسْمُهُ جَابِرٌ" — alternate reading "Jabir".
7. **Children** (p7): "وَلَهُ أَوْلاَدٌ: عَتِيْكٌ، وَعَبْدُ اللهِ، وَأُمُّ ثَابِتٍ" — three children named.
8. **Pact brother** (p8): "آخَى رَسُوْلُ اللهِ ... بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَ خَبَّابِ بنِ الأَرَتِّ" — Prophet established brotherhood with Khabbab ibn al-Aratt.
9. **Badr + conquests + banner** (p9): "شَهِدَ بَدْراً وَالمَشَاهِدَ، وَكَانَتْ إِلَيْهِ رَايَةُ بَنِي مُعَاوِيَةَ بنِ مَالِكٍ يَوْمَ الفَتْحِ" — witnessed Badr and all major scenes; carried banner of Banu Mu'awiyah ibn Malik at conquest of Mecca.
10. **Death year 61 AH** (p10): "قَالَ الوَاقِدِيُّ، وَابْنُ سَعْدٍ، وَخَلِيْفَةُ، وَابْنُ زَبْرٍ، وَابْنُ مَنْدَةَ: تُوُفِّيَ سَنَةَ إِحْدَى وَسِتِّيْنَ" — al-Waqidi, Ibn Sa'd, Khalifa, Ibn Zabr, Ibn Manda agree on 61 AH.
11. **Age 91** (p11): "قِيْلَ: عَاشَ إِحْدَى وَتِسْعِيْنَ سَنَةً" — reported lifespan of 91 years.
12-14. **Muwatta transmission** (p12-14): Chain through Abd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Jabir ibn Atik, from maternal grandfather Atik ibn al-Harith; Jabir ibn Atik narrates the Prophet visiting Abdullah ibn Thabit — the hadith breaks off mid-sentence at the page foot ("جَاءَ يَعُوْدُ...") and concludes on p. 37.

Page 37 holds the entry's tail (5 paragraphs) before al-Ash'ath's entry (entry 8) begins on the same page:

15. **Hadith tail** (p37-p1): "...عَبْدَ اللهِ بنَ ثَابِتٍ، فَوَجَدَهُ قَدْ غُلِبَ..." — the visit's ending; narrators only, not modeled.
16. **Jabir vs Jabr** (p37-p2): al-Dhahabi's own verdict — "الصَّحِيْحُ أَنَّ جَابِرَ بنَ عَتِيْكٍ هُوَ صَاحِبُ هَذَا الخَبَرِ، وَصَاحِبُ تَارِيْخِ الوَفَاةِ، وَأَنَّ جَبْراً قَدِيْمُ الوَفَاةِ" — the Muwatta report and the death date belong to Jabir ibn Atik (of Banu Ghanm ibn Salima), and Jabr died early. See Notes.
17. **Paternal uncle** (p37-p3): "وَعَمُّهُمَا: الحَارِثُ بنُ قَيْسِ بنِ هَيْشَةَ الأَوْسِيُّ" — uncle of both men.
18. **Uncle a Badri** (p37-p4): "بَدْرِيٌّ جَلِيْلٌ..." — counted by al-Waqidi and Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Umarah; no Ibn Uqbah/Ishaq/Abu Mashar listing.
19. **Variant nasab** (p37-p5): "قَالَ ابْنُ إِسْحَاقَ، وَأَبُو مَعْشَرٍ: جَبْرُ بنُ عَتِيْكِ بنِ الحَارِثِ بنِ قَيْسِ بنِ هَيْشَةَ" — inserts al-Harith between Atik and Qays, against the entry header.

## Notes
- Two competing death years (42 AH vs 61 AH) both recorded as separate claims; 61 AH carried as the catalog value with both claims cited, both now marked DISPUTED. The entry's own tail (p. 37) has al-Dhahabi reassigning "تاريخ الوفاة" to Jabir ibn Atik and calling Jabr "قديم الوفاة" (dead long before), explicitly placing Jabir in بني غنم بن سلمة — a different sub-lineage from this entry's بني معاوية بن مالك, so this reads as al-Dhahabi disambiguating two similarly-named men rather than a transcription variant. The 61 AH claim's citations now include that verdict; the catalog keeps 61 AH as the recorded value pending a dedicated Jabir ibn Atik entry, with both supporting and contradicting evidence visible in the claim.
- Name variant "Jabir" (p6) is resolved by that same tail verdict: Jabr and Jabir are two men, not two readings — Jabir of Banu Ghanm ibn Salima owns the hadith and the death date. No name claim authored; lifespan 91 years (p11) likewise stays in the source pages only, since the catalog holds no age field and the tail suggests the date belongs to Jabir.
- Ibn Ishaq's and Abu Mashar's variant nasab (inserting al-Harith between Atik and Qays) kept as a DISPUTED fullName claim; the header nasab stays the carried value, and the SON edge to atik-ibn-qais-al-ansari follows the header.
- The Muwatta transmission chain (p36 p12-14, concluding p37-p1) is not modeled — narrators only, no profile fields or relations. The uncle's Badri standing (p37-p4) is about the uncle and stays in the source pages until he has his own entry.
- Children atik-ibn-jabr, abdullah-ibn-jabr, umm-thabit-bint-jabr and uncle al-harith-ibn-qais-ibn-hayshah-al-awsi are new subjects not yet in catalog.
