# عبيدة بن الحارث — سير أعلام النبلاء، ج١، ص ٢٥٦

## القراءة

Entry 45 of سير أعلام النبلاء (volume 4, printed page 256, Shamela page 1682). The entry is a single page in the section شهداء بئر معونة. It opens with the full nasab, notes the mother is from Thaqif, and describes Ubaydah as one of the early converts, older than the Prophet by ten years. He migrated with his brother al-Tufayl and Husayn. He was tall and handsome, of high standing with the Prophet. He dueled Utbah ibn Rabi'ah at Badr — they exchanged blows, Ali and Hamza struck Utbah and killed him, and Ubaydah was carried from the field with his last breath, dying at al-Safra' in the last ten days of Ramadan, year 2. The Prophet had appointed him over sixty riders from the Muhajirun and gave him the first banner appointed in Islam; he met Quraysh under Abu Sufyan at Thaniyyat al-Marrah, the first fighting in Islam.
