# Summary: عمير بن سعد الأنصاري (Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', vol 5, pp. 103-105)

## Source
**Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'** by al-Dhahabi, vol 5 (سير أعلام النبلاء ج٢), pp. 103-105 (entry 12). Edition: دار الرسالة, 3rd ed. 1405/1985. Digital: المكتبة الشاملة (shamela.ws/book/10906/2083 for p. 103, /2084 for p. 104, /2085 for p. 105). Shamela's الجزء ٢ is this edition's volume 5 (its الجزء count skips the two sira volumes and the caliph volume bound before the Siyar proper).

## Extracted Content
The entry spans three printed pages. Page 103 opens mid-page with the tail of al-Abbas's poem, then Umayr's header; page 104 carries the reports and narrators; page 105 closes the entry (governorship tail, sayings about him) before Abu Sufyan's entry (entry 13) begins on the same page:

- **Header** (103-p9..p11): "١٢ - عُمَيْرُ بنُ سَعْدٍ الأَنْصَارِيُّ الأَوْسِيُّ الزَّاهِدُ" — "نَسيجُ وَحْدِهِ" — "لَهُ حَدِيْثٌ وَاحِدٌ".
- **Narrators** (104-p1): Abu Talhah al-Khawlani, Rashid ibn Sa'd, Habib ibn Ubayd transmit from him; the isnad's Abu Sinan (Isa ibn Sinan) is graded weak in the footnote — chains stay in the source, no nodes.
- **Conquest and governorship** (104-p2): witnessed the conquest of al-Sham; governed Damascus and Hims for Umar.
- **The mangy-horse hadith** (104-p3..p7): isnad then matn — "لا عدوى ولا طيرة ولا هامة" — a narration, not a profile value; stays in the source.
- **Al-Qaddah's report** (104-p8..p9): "صَحِبَ عُمَيْرُ بنُ سَعْدِ بنِ شُهَيْدٍ النَّبِيَّ وَلَمْ يَشْهَدْ شَيْئاً مِنَ المَشَاهِدِ" — companionship with no Prophetic-era battle attendance; he is the one who reported Julas ibn Suwayd's words to the Prophet, an orphan in Julas's care.
- **Hims governorship tail** (105-p1..p4): Umar employed him over Hims and he was of the zuhhad; his tenure followed Ibn Hidhyam's; after Sa'id ibn Amir's death he shared al-Sham with Muawiyah until Umar was killed; Uthman then joined al-Sham under Muawiyah and removed Umayr.
- **Ibn Umar's praise** (105-p5): via Umayr's son Abd al-Rahman — "ما كان من المسلمين رجل من الصحابة أفضل من أبيك".
- **Umar's admiration** (105-p6, Ibn Sirin): Umar named him "نسيج وحده" out of admiration and once sent him over an army.
- **Zuhhad list** (105-p7, al-Mufaddal al-Ghalabi): "زهاد الأنصار ثلاثة: أبو الدرداء وشداد بن أوس وعمير بن سعد".
- **Bibliographic close** (105-p8): Ibn Asakir covered his reports fully.

## Notes
- The worktree draft covered only printed pp. 103-104 and is superseded here: p. 105 (entry tail with the governorship detail, Ibn Umar's praise, Umar's naming, the zuhhad list, and the son) was never transcribed there, and its page/volume metadata confused Shamela ids for printed pages.
- No battle participation is claimed: the entry explicitly says he witnessed none of the Prophet's campaigns (104-p8); the conquest of al-Sham is a post-Prophetic campaign the catalog does not model as a battle, so it lives in virtues wording.
- Guardianship (orphan in Julas's care), the hadith matn/isnad, and Julas himself stay in the source pages — narrators and non-subjects, no nodes or edges.
- Son Abd al-Rahman ibn Umayr ibn Sa'd is a new subject not yet in catalog. Footnote (١) on p. 105 warns "سعد" was corrupted to "سعيد" in print in this report's chain; the transcription follows Shamela's corrected reading.
