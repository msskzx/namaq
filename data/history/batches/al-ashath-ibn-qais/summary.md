# Al-Ash'ath ibn Qais — extraction summary

**Source**: سير أعلام النبلاء (Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'), edition vol. 5 (Siyar juz 2), pp. 37–43
**Extracted**: 2026-10-01 from shamela.ws/book/10906/2017–2023
**Subject**: الأشعث بن قيس بن معدي كرب الكندي (Al-Ash'ath ibn Qais)
**Subject kind**: PERSON

## Scope finding

In scope. The entry states `لَهُ صُحْبَةٌ، وَرِوَايَةٌ` outright (5/38-p6), sits
in the الصحابة run (entry 8, between Jabr ibn Atik and Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah),
and the edition's own source apparatus lists him in الاستيعاب, أسد الغابة and
الإصابة (notes on p. 37). His ridda during the apostasy wars and reconciliation
under Abu Bakr — which is why his companionship is contested elsewhere — is
recorded plainly in the claims' supporting pages (5/39-p4, 5/40-p4: `إِنِّي
ارْتَدَدْتُ`), not smoothed over. Per AGENTS.md a contested Companion is taken
in and the contest recorded.

## Completeness

The entry runs pp. 37–42 plus a three-paragraph tail on p. 43 (his son Muhammad
and grandson Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Ash'ath, ending `وَهَلَكَ`), before the Hatib
heading. An earlier transcription of this entry covered only pp. 37–42; the p.
43 tail is extracted here for the first time.

## Notes

- Death: `قالوا: توفي سنة أربعين، وزاد بعضهم: بعد علي بأربعين ليلة` (still 40),
  `وقيل: عاش ثلاثا وستين سنة`, and Ibn Sa'd places his death in Kufa while
  al-Hasan was there reconciling with Mu'awiyah, with al-Hasan praying over him.
  The year carried is 40; the alternatives stay in the pages for review.
- `sex` stays `legacy-unreviewed`: the entry never states it plainly, and the
  Abu Bardah batch left it the same way.
- The Qais-side `FATHER` edge to al-Ash'ath is promoted to this batch's father
  claim (same fact, other side); his sex and the `FATHER` edge to Qutaylah stay
  `legacy-unreviewed` — this entry is silent on both.
- Children (Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath) and the unnamed daughter under al-Hasan are
  left unmodeled: no checklist item covers them and no catalog person exists.
