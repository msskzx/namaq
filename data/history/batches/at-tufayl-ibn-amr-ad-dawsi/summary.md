## Notes

- The entry runs printed pp. 344–347. Page 347 is shared: the Tufayl tail (`4/347-p1`, on his son Amr and his father counting among the Companions) sits above the بلال بن رباح heading, and the store page holds the whole printed page.
- The nasab chain is short and the heading carries the nisba, so `fullName` keeps الدوسي while `tribalAffiliation` records الدوسي، الأزدي from the heading and `دَوْسٌ بَطْنٌ مِنَ الأَزْدِ`.
- The son Amr (عمرو) and the wife stay unnamed/unstoried in the model: Amr has no catalog entry, so no FATHER edge is authored; both stay in the store pages.
- The father's Islam (`أَيْ بُنَيَّ! دِيْنِي دِيْنُكَ`) is in the store text; the father's module is untouched and stays legacy until his own entry is read.
- No Yamama battle exists in the catalog, so the martyrdom is recorded as `placeOfDeathArabic` (اليمامة) the way the عباد batch did, with the narrative in virtues.
- The three absences come from one remark (`وَسَبَقَتْنِي بَدْرٌ وَأُحُدٌ وَالخَنْدَقُ`), which is what ADR 0013 asks for before recording any absence.
