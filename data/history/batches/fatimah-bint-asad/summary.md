# فاطمة بنت أسد

## Scope

This batch records al-Dhahabi's entry for Fatimah bint Asad. The entry identifies her as Ali ibn Abi Talib's mother, one of the earliest emigrant women, and a Companion. It also records her Hashimite lineage and the Prophet's care for her at her burial.

The account is on printed page 118 of volume 2 of *Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'*, which is volume 5 in the digital edition's complete set. The extraction stops at `118-p10`. Paragraphs `118-p11` and `118-p12`, and the final notes block, belong to the following entry for Fatimah bint Muhammad and were removed.

## Catalog changes

The existing catalog person remains the subject of record. The batch replaces the legacy markers on her name, sex, Companion title, and father relation with citations. It adds the virtues stated in this entry and a cited mother relation to Ali ibn Abi Talib. The source does not state a kunya, physical description, husband, or siblings.

The scoped legacy ledger also lists two values on Asad ibn Hashim and eighteen on Ali ibn Abi Talib because the new claims point to them. This entry supports Fatimah's daughter and mother relations, whose reciprocal graph edges are generated from her catalog declarations. It does not support the other legacy profile values and relations on Asad or Ali, so those remain unchanged.
