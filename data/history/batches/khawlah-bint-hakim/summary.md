# خولة بنت حكيم

Companion index checklist line 169. Source: سير أعلام النبلاء (ط الرسالة), entry
38 in the section على "الصحابة" (فصل: أزواج النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم اللواتي
عُرض عليهن الزواج)، shamela.ws book 10906, pages 2240-2241 (printed vol. 5 /
ص٢٦٠-٢٦١).

## Which خولة

Islamweb's companion index lists one entry titled "خولة" at this position,
directly between قتيلة (entry 37, أخت الأشعث بن قيس) and جويرية أم المؤمنين
(entry 39). The Siyar's own heading for this entry reads "٣٨ - خَوْلَةُ بِنْتُ
حَكِيْمٍ" — Khawlah bint Hakim, one of the women whose marriage to the Prophet
ﷺ is reported and then deferred. This matches the task's expected identity.

## What the entry says

Two short paragraphs, split across the printed page break (260/261):

- A chain of narration naming her: عمارة بن راشد، عن علي بن زيد، عن ابن
  المسيب، عنها.
- وكان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم تزوجها؛ فأرجأها فيمن أرجأ من نسائه — the
  Prophet ﷺ married her, then deferred her among the wives he deferred.

That is the entire entry: no kunya, no physical description, no reported
virtues/sayings beyond the marriage-and-deferral report, and no siblings —
all four marked `notInSource` on the account.

## Scope call: no "أم المؤمنين" title

Unlike جويرية, whose Siyar heading itself reads "جُوَيْرِيَةُ أُمُّ
المُؤْمِنِيْنَ", Dhahabi's heading for this entry is plain "خَوْلَةُ بِنْتُ
حَكِيْمٍ" with no "أم المؤمنين" honorific, even though the same paragraph
reports the Prophet ﷺ married her. The catalog entry therefore carries the
`WIFE`/`HUSBAND` relation to `prophet-muhammad` (the marriage is explicitly
stated), but does not add the `mother-of-believers` title assignment that
other consummated wives (Aisha, Hafsa, Sawdah, ...) carry on
`prophet-muhammad.ts` — the source itself withholds that title here, and
inventing it would go beyond what the entry states. `companion` is carried
as `legacy-unreviewed`, following the precedent on `as-saib-ibn-uthman.ts`:
the entry never calls her صحابية outright either.

No father relation was declared for "بنت حكيم": no catalog person named
`hakim` matching her father exists yet, so the nasab is captured only in
`fields.fullName`.
