# Summary: أبو ذر الغفاري (Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', vol 5, pp. 46-78)

## Source
**Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'** by al-Dhahabi, vol 5 (سير أعلام النبلاء ج٢), pp. 46-78 (entry 10). Edition: دار الرسالة, 3rd ed. 1405/1985. Digital: المكتبة الشاملة (shamela.ws/book/10906/2026 for p. 46 through /2058 for p. 78). Shamela's الجزء ٢ is this edition's volume 5 (its الجزء count skips the two sira volumes and the caliph volume bound before the Siyar proper).

## Extracted Content
The entry spans 33 printed pages, the longest Companion entry extracted so far. Page 46 opens cleanly with Abu Dharr's header (no shared previous-entry tail); page 78 closes the entry (death narrative tail, two closing reports) before al-Abbas's entry (entry 11) begins on the same page — the account keeps p. 78 through "بَلْ كُنْتُ أُصَلِّي" and drops the Abbas header and his bibliography note:

- **Header and nasab** (46-p1..p5): "١٠ - أَبُو ذَرٍّ جُنْدُبُ بنُ جُنَادَةَ الغِفَارِيُّ" with three "وقيل" variants (جندب بن سكن؛ برير بن جنادة؛ برير بن عبد الله — plus يزيد بن جنادة and برير on p. 49) and al-Dimyati's extended chain up to Kinana. Only the header form is carried as `fullName`; the variants stay in the pages.
- **Standing** (46-p6..p9): among السابقين الأولين, "خامس خمسة" (with "رابع الإسلام / ربع الإسلام" in his own words on pp. 55-56); sent back to his people by the Prophet's order, emigrated after the Hijra, fought alongside him; gave fatwa under Abu Bakr, Umar and Uthman.
- **Badr and Jerusalem** (47-p2, p5): "فاتته بدر" (Abu Dawud) — an explicit absence; "شهد فتح بيت المقدس مع عمر".
- **Appearance** (47-p3; 50-p2): "آدم ضخم جسيم كث اللحية"؛ "طوال آدم أبيض الرأس واللحية" (with "أسود كث الشعر" in a later report on p. 74, left in the pages).
- **Islam story** (50-54, Muslim's narration): leaves Ghifar with his brother Unays and their mother; Unays the poet checks the Prophet first; thirty days on Zamzam water alone; "كنت أول من حياه بتحية الإسلام"; ordered to conceal and return, proclaims it before Quraysh instead; Ghifar and Aslam accept Islam ("غفار غفر الله لها، وأسلم سالمها الله").
- **Tabuk** (56): his camel fails ("أبطأت في غزوة تبوك من عجف بعيري"), he shoulders his baggage and catches up on foot; the Prophet's verdict: "رحم الله أبا ذر، يمشي وحده، ويموت وحده، ويبعث وحده".
- **Hunayn** (56-p3, al-Waqidi): carried Ghifar's banner.
- **Praise and counsel** (58-64): "ما أقلت الغبراء ولا أظلت الخضراء من رجل أصدق لهجة من أبي ذر"؛ "من سره أن ينظر إلى زهد عيسى فلينظر إلى أبي ذر"؛ the five counsels and the seven counsels (love of the poor, truth though bitter, fearing no blame); Ali on his knowledge ("وعاء ملئ علماً"); the Prophet sought him out when present and missed him when absent.
- **Hoarding dispute and Rabdha** (63-71): preaches "بشر الكنازين" against hoarders; Muawiya complains he "أفسد الشام"; Uthman summons him ("اطلبوه / اقدم عليّ"); he repeatedly *asks permission* for al-Rabdha ("ائذن لي") and it is granted ("فأذن له") — alongside Ibn Ishaq's "نفى عثمان أبا ذر إلى الربذة" wording at the death narrative. Both wordings are preserved in the pages; no claim adjudicates between استأذن and نفى.
- **Death** (74-78): "مات سنة اثنتين وثلاثين" (al-Fallas, al-Haytham ibn Adi and others; "يقال: مات في ذي الحجة"); dies at al-Rabdha with only his wife and ghulam beside him; Ibn Masud's party finds them; the Ansari with his mother's spinning shrouds him (Hujr ibn al-Adbar and Malik al-Ashtar among the witnesses); "تمشي وحدك، وتموت وحدك، وتبعث وحدك" (split across pp. 77-78, cited as a range).
- **Household** (74-p10; 76-77): "خلف بنتاً له، فضمها عثمان إلى عياله" — an unnamed daughter, kept in Uthman's household; the wife is never named (امرأته؛ أم ذر in her own report on p. 72; "امرأة له سوداء مشعثة" on p. 73). No wife or daughter can be given a slug, so no relation is authored.
- **Hadith count** (75-p5): 281 hadiths, 12 agreed upon, 2 al-Bukhari alone, 19 Muslim alone — a count the model has no shape for; stays in the pages.

## Not in Source
- **wives**: the wife appears throughout the death narrative but is never named (امرأته؛ أم ذر؛ امرأة له سوداء مشعثة), and the daughter on p. 74 is unnamed too — no HUSBAND/WIFE relation can be authored.

These are recorded in `account.notInSource`. The entry was read in full across all 33 pages, so the absences are asserted over the complete entry.
