# Summary: العباس بن عبد المطلب (Siyar A'lam al-Nubala', vol 5, pp. 78-102)

## Source
**Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'** by al-Dhahabi, vol 5 (سير أعلام النبلاء ج٢), pp. 78-102 (entry 11). Edition: دار الرسالة, 3rd ed. 1405/1985. Digital: المكتبة الشاملة (shamela.ws/book/10906/2058 for p. 78 through /2082 for p. 102). ## Scope
Al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, the Prophet's paternal uncle, is a well-known صحابي; entry 11 sits inside Shamela's "تابع: الطبقة الأولى - الصحابة". In scope per the companion-only rule.

## Extracted Content
- **Opening** (78-p7..p8): "١١ - العَبَّاسُ عَمُّ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ" — no nasab chain is ever stated. "قيل: إنه أسلم قبل الهجرة وكتم إسلامه، وخرج مع قومه إلى بدر فأسر يومئذ، فادعى أنه مسلم، فالله أعلم."
- **Pre-Fath standing** (79-p1): not among the طلقاء — he had come to the Prophet before the conquest; his protection (أجار) of Abu Sufyan ibn Harb is the proof.
- **Appearance** (79-p7..p9, 80-p1, 95-p6): al-Kalbi's "شريفا مهيبا عاقلا جميلا أبيض بضا له ضفيرتان معتدل القامة"; Dhahabi's own "من أطول الرجال وأحسنهم صورة وأبهاهم وأجهرهم صوتا"; "تام الشكل جهوري الصوت جدا" (hence the Prophet ordering him to shout يوم حنين).
- **Badr** (81-83): standing like an idol with tears (Umarah's report); captured (three capture reports: Abu al-Yusr / an Ansari / an angel's aid); ordered to ransom himself, "ابن أخيك عقيلا"، نوفل بن الحارث، and his ally Utbah ibn Jahdam; the twenty ounces of gold and Umm al-Fadl's secret deposit; Anfal 70 revealed about the captives; highest ransom at Badr (100 ounces of gold); the Prophet sleepless at his uncle's groaning in bonds.
- **Second Aqaba** (85-p12, 86-p8, 96-p5): came alone with his nephew, spoke first to the Seventy, and the Prophet pledged them with Abbas holding his hand confirming the pledge.
- **Fath Makkah** (87-p9, 79-p1): joined the Prophet after Khaybar ("ثم خرج معه إلى فتح مكة").
- **Hunayn** (88-p2, 95-p6): held the Prophet's mule's bridle at the rout and stood firm till victory; ordered to cry "يا أصحاب الشجرة".
- **Manaqeb**: "عم الرجل صنو أبيه" (twice, two occasions); "العباس مني وأنا منه"; "أجود قريش كفا وأوصلها"; the cloak du'a for him and his sons (إسناده جيد); Umar's istisqa through him in the year of al-Ramada (one report graded صحيح); the Prophet honored him as a son honors his father; Umar allotted him 12,000; Ali kissing his hand and foot; his pre-Islamic generosity to Banu Hashim and neighbors.
- **Household** (84-p11..p12, 85-p4..p5): seven sons and Umm Habiba by Umm al-Fadl Lubabah al-Hilaliyyah; Kathir, Tammam and Umaymah by an umm walad; al-Harith by Hujaylah bint Jundab al-Tamimiyyah — ten children in all.
- **Death** (97-p4..p7, 100-p4..p10, 101): died 32 AH, Uthman prayed over him, buried in al-Baqi' under the great dome of his Abbasid descendants; Khalifah and others say 34, al-Mada'ini says 33. Funeral crowds, Hashimites descending into the grave, the washing by Ali, Ibn Abbas, Qutham and Ubaydullah.
- **Closing reports** (97-p11..p13, 98-99, 102): the "أنا أسن منه" exchange; al-Waqidi's Meccan-correspondent account (graded ضعيف) with Dhahabi's rejoinder "والظاهر أن إسلامه كان بعد بدر"; the/withheld-hijra letters (one graded واه); "العباس مني وأنا منه" again; the Thurayya progeny reports (one موضوع, one رواه الحاكم with عبيد غير ثقة); Khuraym hearing the praise poem at the return from Tabuk.

## Legacy values visited
- **sex, virtues, companion title, SON→Abd al-Muttalib, HUSBAND→Umm al-Fadl**: promoted to the cited claims above.
- **fullName** ("العباس بن عبد المطلب بن هاشم القرشي الهاشمي"): stays legacy. The entry states "العباس بن عبد المطلب" once (the funeral announcement, 100-p9) and names his mother (84-p8), but never gives the بن هاشم chain or a tribal affiliation — promoting the full string would cite what the source does not state.
- **Khaybar participation** (seed-carried, battles/khaybar.ts): the entry itself refutes it — Ibn Sa'd calls the Khaybar-share report "هذا وهم" (87-p8). The row is removed rather than left standing uncited.
- **Tabuk participation** (seed-carried, battles/tabuk.ts): the entry's only Tabuk link is Khuraym hearing Abbas recite the praise poem at the Prophet's return from Tabuk (102-p11..p12), transmitted through Ubayd ibn Abi Qurrah, whom the entry itself grades غير ثقة (102-p10). Not enough to sustain a participation edge; the row is removed rather than left standing uncited.
- **Uhud/Khandaq**: "ثم لا ذكر له يوم أحد، ولا يوم الخندق" (96-p7) is Dhahabi noting that no report places him there, not a remarked absence — no ABSENT_FROM is authored, and the seed never placed him there either.
