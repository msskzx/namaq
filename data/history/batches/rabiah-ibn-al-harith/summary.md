# ربيعة بن الحارث — batch summary

## What the source says

Entry 46 of سير أعلام النبلاء (printed pages 257–258, Shamela 1683–1684, volume 4).
The entry opens the أعيان البدريين section. ربيعة بن الحارث بن عبد المطلب بن هاشم
الهاشمي، يكنى أبا أروى. The entry lists his children, tells of his son آدَم who was
killed as a toddler and the Prophet's hadith about him, notes that ربيعة was older than
his uncle العباس by two years and was absent from Badr (in Sham), tells of his
conversion during the الخندق period when العباس and نوفل convinced him to join them,
records the Prophet's praise of him (نعم العبد ربيعة بن الحارث), the Prophet's gift of
100 wasq from Khaybar yearly, his presence at the Conquest and Hunayn, his house in
Medina, his death during عمر's caliphate, and his trade partnership with عثمان.
